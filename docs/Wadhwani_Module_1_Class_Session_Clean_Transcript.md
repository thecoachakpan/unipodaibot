**Charles Bolton:** Welcome everyone to Module 1 of the Wadhwani Ignite AI Business Model Program! Today we focus on **Problem Identification and Statement Formulation**. 

### The Solution-First Trap
Entrepreneurs frequently fall into the "solution-first trap"—getting infatuated with their AI prototype or product features before deeply understanding the problem. Whether you have an existing MVP or an operating business, set your solution aside today. If a problem is weak, misunderstood, or non-existent, perfecting your AI product only accelerates failure.

### Definition of a Problem vs. Opportunity
- **A Problem** is the gap between a customer's current painful experience and their desired outcome, for which they are actively seeking a remedy and willing to pay.
- **An Opportunity** is NOT the technology (e.g. "AI for retail stores"); it is the measurable value created by eliminating the customer's pain (e.g. "Helping independent retailers eliminate stockouts, prevent cash flow losses, and make timely inventory decisions").

## 3. Step-by-Step Guide to Module 1 Venture Activities

Module 1 consists of 4 lessons and 3 mandatory practical activities under the **Venture Journey** tab:

### Activity 1.1: Core Problem Statement & Validation Data
- **Problem Statement (Max 350 characters):** Articulate the precise problem your startup solves. Focus strictly on who suffers, what happens, and why. **Do NOT mention your solution, product, or AI features.**
- **Validation Data & Source:** Provide third-party research data proving the problem exists (e.g., industry statistics, survey percentages) and cite the exact source publication and year.
- **Program Institution:** Enter `UNDP UniPod AI Program`.

### Activity 1.2: Sector Classification
Select the area, macro-industry, and functional domain aligning with your startup venture (e.g., Area: *Business Services*, Industry: *Professional Business Services*, Domain: *Accounting & Financial Services*).

### Activity 1.3: Stakeholder Impact & Problem Restatement
- **Affected Stakeholders:** Identify primary customers and secondary stakeholders (suppliers, employees).
- **Negative Impact:** Detail measurable financial or operational losses (lost revenue, cash flow pressure, inventory waste).
- **Root Causes:** List structural reasons the problem persists.
- **Team Passion Connection:** Explain why your team is committed to solving this challenge.
- **Condensed Restatement (Max 150 characters):** Sharpen the 350-character problem statement into a focused, high-impact statement under 150 characters.
