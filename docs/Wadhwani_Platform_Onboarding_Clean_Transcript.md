## 1. Welcome & Program Context (Charles Bolton, Jeovaire Umukundwa, Gift Ntuli)

Welcome everyone to the official onboarding for the Wadhwani Foundation Ignite AI Program. Over the next 12 weeks, we will be working together on building viable, market-ready AI business models.

### Key Clarification: Boot Camp vs. 12-Week Wadhwani Ignite Track
* **Generic Boot Camp (4 Weeks):** A short exploratory program covering general startup ideation.
* **12-Week Wadhwani Ignite Program:** An intensive, structured program focused specifically on validating and building an AI-driven venture. Even if you completed the 4-week Boot Camp, **you must enroll in the 12-Week Wadhwani Ignite Africa track** and complete Module 0 baseline tasks.

## 4. Live Case Studies Covered in Session

1. **M-KOPA (Fintech & AI Analytics):**
   - Combines asset financing for smartphones and solar devices with AI credit analytics. Uses payment history to build credit scores for unbanked individuals, supporting ~3,000 staff and ~50,000 sales agents across East & West Africa.
2. **Zipline (Autonomous AI Logistics):**
   - Operates autonomous drones using AI flight navigation and weather adaptation to deliver blood products and vaccines to remote rural clinics in minutes.
3. **Shop Pilot (Charles Bolton's Teaching Case Study):**
   - An AI assistant for informal kiosk owners in Africa. Captures voice notes and receipts to track inventory, forecast cash flow, and prevent stockouts.

## 5. Cleaned Participant Q&A Transcript

### Q1: I completed the 4-week Boot Camp ending this Sunday. Do I need to enroll in this program as well?
**Charles Bolton:** Yes. The 4-week boot camp was a generic exploratory phase. The 12-week Wadhwani Ignite program is the official AI business model building track. You must register on the platform under "Ignite Full Africa" and complete the baseline tasks.

### Q2: What if the system says my account already exists when I try to sign up?
**Charles Bolton:** If you participated in a previous Wadhwani course or boot camp, log in with your existing credentials, navigate to your student dashboard, click "Enroll in Course" or "Add Cohort", and enter your country's cohort code.

### Q3: Why does my Passion CV say "South Africa" when I am located in Rwanda / Nigeria / Kenya / Ethiopia?
**Charles Bolton:** Disregard the "South Africa" label. It is a default system template tag from the platform's initial deployment. All participants across all 21 African countries use this exact same Passion CV module.

### Q4: Can my co-founders and I share a single account on the platform?
**Charles Bolton:** No. Every single team member must create their own individual account with their own email address and complete their own Baseline Survey and Passion CV. We will group and link your venture teams during live class on Tuesday.

### Q5: Can solo founders participate without building a team?
**Charles Bolton:** Yes. Solopreneurs are fully supported and can complete the entire 12-week program individually.

### Q6: Can we bring in team members who did not register in the original UNDP application?
**Charles Bolton:** You are free to collaborate with anyone on your startup product in real life, but only registered participants on the official roster can receive official course certificates and platform access.

### Q7: What about the MIT Universal AI track? How does it connect to Wadhwani?
**Jeovaire Umukundwa / Charles Bolton:** The MIT track provides foundational technical AI skills, while Wadhwani provides AI business model grounding. Access codes for MIT are distributed separately by UNDP UniPod leads. Both tracks run concurrently.

### Q8: Is the program offered in French?
**Charles Bolton:** The live lectures and platform are delivered in English for the 2026 cohort. French-speaking participants are encouraged to use live translation tools (such as Teams translation captions or AI translation tools). An official French Wadhwani platform will launch in 2027.
